Daily Fitness Routine

Follow these six simple exercises to stay active and build strength. Do each exercise with good form and rest for 30 seconds between them.

Jumping Jacks

3 sets of 30 seconds. A quick warm-up that gets your heart pumping.

Push-ups

3 sets of 10 reps. Builds strength in your chest, arms and shoulders.

Squats

3 sets of 15 reps. Strengthens your legs and core.

Plank

3 sets of 30 seconds. Keep your body straight to train your core.

Lunges

3 sets of 12 reps per leg. Improves balance and leg strength.

Burpees

3 sets of 8 reps. A full-body exercise to finish your workout.

Tip: Drink water before and after your workout, and stop if you feel pain.
