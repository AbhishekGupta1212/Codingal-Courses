My Morning School Routine

  1. Wake up and brush my teeth
  2. Take a bath and get dressed in my uniform
  3. Eat a healthy breakfast
  4. Pack my school bag
  5. Travel to school and meet my friends
  6. Attend morning assembly

Things I Want in My Dream School

  • A big library full of exciting books
  • A science lab for fun experiments
  • A large playground and a swimming pool
  • A garden where we can grow plants
  • Computers and robots to learn coding

School Subject Descriptions

Mathematics
Learning numbers, shapes and how to solve problems.
Science
Exploring plants, animals, space and how things work.
English
Reading stories, writing and speaking clearly.
Art
Drawing, painting and creating with colours.
Physical Education
Playing sports and staying fit and healthy.
