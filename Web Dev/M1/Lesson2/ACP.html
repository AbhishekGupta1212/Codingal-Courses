Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The “Missile Man of India” and the “People’s President”

Born on 15 October 1930 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He is one of the people I admire most.

Early Life

Kalam grew up in a modest family in the island town of Rameswaram. As a boy he delivered newspapers to help support his family.

Education

He studied physics and then aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology.

Scientific Career

Kalam worked with DRDO and ISRO for decades. His work in missile development earned him the nickname Missile Man of India.

Space Programme

He played a leading role in India’s satellite launch programme.

ISRO Years

At ISRO he became a project director for a major launch vehicle.

SLV-III

SLV-III was India’s first indigenous satellite launch vehicle.

Rohini Satellite

In 1980, SLV-III placed the Rohini satellite into orbit.

Science Corner

Rockets burn fuel to create thrust. When hydrogen and oxygen burn together, they form water, written as H2O. Einstein’s famous formula for energy is E = mc2.

Words of Wisdom

Kalam believed in constant effort. He said, Excellence is a continuous process and not an accident.

One of his best-known thoughts is about dreams:

Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action.

President of India

From 2002 to 2007, Kalam served as the 11th President of India. He was loved for his simplicity and for meeting students and young people. He received the Bharat Ratna in 1997.

Why I Admire Him

I admire Dr. Kalam because he rose from humble beginnings through hard work, curiosity and humility. His life shows that where you start does not decide where you can reach.
